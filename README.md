# Japanese study

A local study app for a daily Japanese course: vocabulary, grammar, reading, listening, quizzes, and an eye-rest timer.

## Start

On Windows, from the `app` folder:

```bat
StartJapaneseStudy.bat
```

Or:

```bat
py -3 study_server.py
```

The app opens at http://127.0.0.1:8765/. Sign in with an identifier and password. A new identifier starts registration.

## Each day

Open **Today's lesson**. The course covers vocabulary, grammar, sample sentences, reading, listening, and a quiz. Scores stay on this computer, under your account.

The **Eye protection** panel on the right reminds you to look away, blink, and dim the screen. **Easy on eyes** warms the colors.
